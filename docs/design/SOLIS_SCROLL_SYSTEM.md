## 1. Scroll Philosophy: Progressive Revelation

In Solis, scrolling is not merely vertical displacement; it is **chronological and intellectual discovery**. The user is guided through the intellectual philosophy of circadian rhythm, empirical cognitive limits, and the calm mastery of deliberate study.
