# Solis — UX Reference Index

## Overview
This index documents 30+ benchmark interaction design and information architecture references analyzed across leading digital products.

Every reference follows our strict analysis framework:
- **Source & Product**
- **Screen / Flow**
- **What is Excellent**
- **What Solis Should Learn**
- **What Solis Should NOT Copy**
- **Solis Application**

---

## 1. Linear (Desktop & Web)
* **Screen / Flow**: Issue Triage, View Filtering, Command Bar (`Cmd+K`).
* **What is Excellent**: High-density without visual noise. Zero unnecessary borders; hierarchy established entirely through typography, subtle background tints, and exact alignment.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Keyboard-first speed, discrete status indicators, instant feedback on state changes.
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Ultra-dark developer-centric aesthetic; Solis is an editorial learning sanctuary with warm ivory tones.
* **Solis Application**: Task Sanctuary and Quick Search (`Cmd+K`) header interactions.

---

## 2. Sunsama (Web & Desktop)
* **Screen / Flow**: Daily Planning Ritual, Timeboxing, Daily Shutdown / Closure.
* **What is Excellent**: Guided step-by-step ritual (Reflect $\to$ Plan $\to$ Prioritize $\to$ Timebox) that aligns executive function with realistic daily capacity.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Morning arrival intention and evening closure reflection.
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Complex multi-integration calendar sync wizard.
* **Solis Application**: Solis Daily Flow Arrival Scene and Evening Closure & Preparation modal.

---

## 3. Craft Docs (macOS & iOS)
* **Screen / Flow**: Document Canvas, Block Stylings, Folder Organization.
* **What is Excellent**: Content-first editorial layout. Text feels physically elevated and worthy of attention. Generous line-height and beautiful margins.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Bounded reading line length (`760px`), large display serif headlines (`Newsreader`), seamless inline tag management.
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Complex enterprise team workspace hierarchies.
* **Solis Application**: Solis Knowledge Studio writing canvas.

---

## 4. Raycast (macOS)
* **Screen / Flow**: Extensions Grid, Action Menus, Keyboard Shortcuts.
* **What is Excellent**: Absolute interaction clarity. Every keypress has an unambiguous predictable response.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Custom select and dropdown keyboard navigation (`ArrowUp`, `ArrowDown`, `Enter`, `Escape`).
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Floating overlay popovers for entire page navigation.
* **Solis Application**: `<CustomSelect>` and modal keyboard accessibility.

---

## 5. Notion (Desktop & Web)
* **Screen / Flow**: Breadcrumbs, Sub-pages, Multi-column Layouts.
* **What is Excellent**: Flexible spatial hierarchy and nested page breadcrumbs.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Topic-to-Subject relational linkages in Study and Notes.
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Blank-page syndrome and unstructured database tables.
* **Solis Application**: Living syllabus topic hierarchy in Study Studio.

---

## 6. Apple System Applications (macOS & iOS)
* **Screen / Flow**: Apple Notes, Apple Health Trends, Apple Books Reading Environment.
* **What is Excellent**: Restraint, deliberate negative space, natural contrast, and accessible typography.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Tactile scale feedback (`transform: scale(0.98)` on press) and calm typographic rhythm.
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Pure flat white backgrounds without atmospheric warmth.
* **Solis Application**: Warm Ivory canvas (`#FAF8F5`) and tactile button micro-interactions.

---

## 7. Reflect Notes (Web & macOS)
* **Screen / Flow**: Daily Journaling Canvas, Backlink Visualizer.
* **What is Excellent**: The feeling that writing is an act of memory retrieval and capture. Discrete auto-saving status without popup noise.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Real-time `Auto-saved` state indicator next to the trash action.
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Dense graph visualizer node network.
* **Solis Application**: Knowledge Studio auto-save status and session insight tagging.

---

## 8. Bear App (macOS & iOS)
* **Screen / Flow**: Markdown Writing Canvas, Three-Pane Column Structure.
* **What is Excellent**: Unmatched typography rendering and peaceful writing environment.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Borderless distraction-free textarea with generous line-height (1.8).
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Pure markdown raw markup showing symbols everywhere.
* **Solis Application**: Editorial body textarea in Knowledge Studio.

---

## 9. Readwise & Reader (Web & Mobile)
* **Screen / Flow**: Daily Review Queue, Highlight Funnel.
* **What is Excellent**: Continuity of learning—presenting ideas when they are fresh.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Continuity Memory Strip derived from real data ("Continue where you left off").
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Automated flashcard deluge.
* **Solis Application**: Daily Flow Continuity Strip and Study living queue.

---

## 10. Heptabase (Web & Desktop)
* **Screen / Flow**: Visual Whiteboard, Card Synthesizer.
* **What is Excellent**: Visual sense-making of complex subjects.
* **What Solis Should Learn**: Subject Worlds—distinct atmospheric identity for disciplines (Coral, Amber, Lavender, Sage).
* **What Solis Should NOT Copy**: Infinite 2D zoom canvas.
* **Solis Application**: Subject World tiles and topic mastery states.
