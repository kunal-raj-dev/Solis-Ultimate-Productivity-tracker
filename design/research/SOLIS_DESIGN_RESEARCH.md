# Solis — Comprehensive Design Research & Synthesis

## 1. Executive Summary
This document synthesizes our multi-category external research across **Product UX, Writing Environments, Focus & Rituals, Learning Systems, Daily Planning, Visual Art Direction, Real Production Interfaces, Commercial Experience, and Mobile Patterns**.

---

## 2. Key Synthesis Findings

### A. The "Anti-Dashboard" Imperative
Every analyzed benchmark (Linear, Craft, Sunsama, Endel) succeeds by **subtracting chrome** and establishing a singular mental space. Dashboard widgets and card walls create cognitive fragmentation. Solis replaces widgets with a **vertical narrative journey** (Daily Flow).

### B. Continuity vs. Artificial Intelligence
Users do not want fake AI summaries or generative noise. They want **product memory**—a system that remembers their last studied topic, their next planned block, their current focus thread, and their distilled notes.

### C. Emotion in Productivity
Standard productivity tools feel cold and corporate. Solis introduces warmth through:
* Warm Ivory base canvas (`#FAF8F5`)
* High-contrast `Newsreader` display serif typography
* Acoustic 528Hz singing bowl resonance
* Subject Worlds ambient lighting
* Evening closure reflection ritual
